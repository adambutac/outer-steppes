## Context

The problem, and the facts the decision depends on: platform behavior, constraints, what was observed and when. State them here, as of today; don't link outside this folder. Keep every section short (README.md, Standing on its own).

## Options

### Option A

What it is, what it gives, what it costs.

### Option B

The same for each alternative that was seriously considered, including keeping things as they are.

## Decision

What was chosen, in a sentence or two, and the main reason it beat the other options.

## Consequences

<!-- Lasting effects of the decision, not work items: what's built and by whom lives in Linear and the PR. -->

- What becomes easier, and what becomes harder.
- Risks or costs accepted on purpose, and who carries them.
- What changed in DESIGN.md, README.md or code to put this in place.

## Revisit if

The conditions that would reopen this decision, such as a platform change, a new threat or a different user base. "None expected" is a valid answer.
